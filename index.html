Inspection: Look for symmetry, scars, accessory muscle use, cyanosis.
Palpation: Feel for chest expansion, tracheal deviation, crepitus.
Percussion: Tap for resonance (Normal, Dull=fluid/consolidation, Hyper=air/pneumothorax).
Auscultation: Listen to all zones for air entry, wheeze, crackles, or rubs.

10 Rules of a Normal ECG

>200ms = Heart Block. <120ms = WPW.
≥120ms = LBBB/RBBB or VT.
Checks axis.
Discordance suggests ischemia/BBB.
If upright, suspect swapped leads.
Poor progression = old MI / LVH.
Elevation = STEMI. Depression = Ischemia.
Inverted = ectopic atrial rhythm.
>1mm wide OR >25% depth = dead tissue.
Inversion = ischemia, strain, PE.

📈 Interactive Rhythm Assistant (Tap to open)

1. Rate:
Count QRS complexes in a 10s strip x6.
2. Regularity:
Are R-R intervals equally spaced?
3. P Waves:
Best seen in Lead II or V1.
4. PR Interval / AV Blocks:
Start of P to start of QRS.
AV Block Guide:
• 1st Degree: PR >5 small squares, but every P has a QRS.
• Mobitz I (Wenckebach): PR gets progressively longer until QRS dropped.
• Mobitz II: PR is constant, but QRS randomly drops. High risk.
• 3rd Degree: P and QRS beat independently. P rate > QRS rate. Needs pacing.
5. QRS Width:
Start of Q to end of S.
BBB Guide (Leads V1 & V6):
• LBBB (WiLLiaM): 'W' in V1, 'M' in V6. New LBBB + CP = STEMI.
• RBBB (MaRRoW): 'M' in V1, 'W' in V6.

3. Have you been able to have a rational conversation with the patient about the pros and cons of what is proposed?
4. Do you think the patient can retain information for long enough to make the decision?
5. Is the patient able to explain their decision using their own words (in a way appropriate for them)?
