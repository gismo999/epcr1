10 Rules of a Normal ECG

>200ms = Heart Block. <120ms = WPW.
≥120ms = LBBB/RBBB or VT.
Checks axis.
Discordance suggests ischemia/BBB.
If upright, suspect swapped leads.
Poor progression = old MI / LVH.
Elevation = STEMI. Depression = Ischemia.
Inverted = ectopic atrial rhythm.
>1mm wide OR >25% depth = dead tissue.
Inversion = ischemia, strain, PE.

📈 Interactive Rhythm Assistant (Tap to open)

1. Rate:
Count QRS complexes in a 10s strip x6.
2. Regularity:
Are R-R intervals equally spaced?
3. P Waves:
Best seen in Lead II or V1.
4. PR Interval / AV Blocks:
Start of P to start of QRS.
AV Block Guide:
• 1st Degree: PR >5 small squares, but every P has a QRS.
• Mobitz I: PR gets progressively longer until QRS dropped.
• Mobitz II: PR is constant, but QRS randomly drops. High risk.
• 3rd Degree: P and QRS beat independently. P rate > QRS rate.
5. QRS Width:
Start of Q to end of S.
BBB Guide (Leads V1 & V6):
• LBBB (WiLLiaM): 'W' in V1, 'M' in V6. New LBBB + CP = STEMI.
• RBBB (MaRRoW): 'M' in V1, 'W' in V6.
